# Welding Gas Wallet icon assets

`play-store-icon-512.png` is the Play Console icon. The same approved cylinder artwork is consumed by `app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/welding_wallet_icon.png` for the launcher and splash drawable wrappers.

The icon uses the product blue (`#1758B7`) and a white welding-gas-cylinder mark. It contains no text, letters, third-party branding, or platform corner mask.
